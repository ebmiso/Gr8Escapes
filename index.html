A FEW THINGS BEFORE YOU GO

Make yourself at ease.

How do I check availability and book?+

Choose a home and select “Check availability.” You’ll open its Houfy listing, where you can enter your dates, review the total price and policies, and complete your reservation.

Which stay is best for a larger group?+

Emerald, Moonstone, and Season 4 each sleep up to 16. Gr8 Epic Escape pairs two homes for up to 32 guests. Mountain Escape has four bedrooms and sleeps 10–12. Review each listing for sleeping arrangements.

Do all homes have the same amenities?+

No. Each home has its own personality and amenities. Compare the property cards, then check the full listing for details, including sauna, theater, and arcade availability.

Where can I find house rules and cancellation terms?+

Current house rules, fees, check-in information, and cancellation terms are provided in the individual booking listings. Please review them before reserving.
